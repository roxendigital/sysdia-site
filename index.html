What it does

Six things, done well.

Sysdia is a record-keeping tool for people who measure their blood pressure at home.

Log in seconds

Big keys make entering your numbers quick. Pulse and a note are optional.

Today, at a glance

Your latest reading, the week's chart and your streak, all on one screen.

Every reading, labelled

Each reading shows its category, using the American Heart Association ranges. A label describes one reading and is not a diagnosis.

Watch your trend

Switch between week, month and longer views to see how your daily averages move.

Gentle reminders

Checking at the same times each day gives your doctor the clearest picture. Lock screen alerts never show your numbers.

Take it to your doctor

Share a clean PDF report with your averages, chart and every reading.

Free and Pro

Free to use. Upgrade once.

Logging, reminders and backup are free, always. Pro adds more history, more reminders and longer reports.

Pay once, keep it.

About the price of a coffee, one time. No monthly fees and no subscription. The price shows in your own currency in the app store.
